Interpret these measurements
Full repair means every protected check passed. Partial correctness is reported even when the repair is incomplete. Return uses v7 rewards relative to the initial candidate. Signal share counts eight-attempt groups containing both successes and failures, before filtering. It does not establish that training improves a model.
For statistical comparisons, collect enough distinct worlds, pair models on the same tasks, and cluster repeated attempts by task. Do not treat eight attempts on one world as eight independent worlds.
