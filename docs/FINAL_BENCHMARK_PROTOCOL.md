# Final baseline protocol

Frozen before application targets or model decisions are sampled. No RL weight updates are authorized until the GPU becomes available.

- Profiles: GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6.1 Terra and GPT-5.6 Sol via Codex sign-in; Claude Sonnet 5.5 via Anthropic on OpenRouter; paid Qwen3.8 27B FP8 via Chutes on OpenRouter. Neutral preflight must pass first. No model or provider fallback after task calls begin.
- Target: 120 distinct worlds per profile, 30 per family, ten per difficulty level. Same worlds across profiles, one independent trajectory per profile/world. Whole benchmark: 600 planned attempts. Planned, access-interrupted and budget-censored records are not completed repair scores.
- Known-family track: drag, linked brushing and contact dynamics with new compositions, private manifests and new scene ranges. Three train-family mechanisms remain development-known; this track is not whole-family holdout.
- Held-out-family primary track: 30 detection-overlay worlds with independently sampled public raw rows/geometry and private pipeline configurations. Detector postprocessing is excluded from the train generator. This is procedural imagery, not real detector performance or evidence about model pretraining contamination.
- Levels: easy = one added rule / baseline candidate / 48 actions; medium = two rules / baseline / 32; hard = three rules / starter / 24. Detection has six pipeline settings at all levels; its budget varies 48/32/24. These are joint difficulty conditions, not isolated causal interventions.
- Every generated program must agree with an independently written reference, be valid in the bounded interpreter, expose every rule on at least two checks, avoid observation aliases and fit a declared whole-catalogue ideal policy with two actions spare. This finite-program prior is a strong solvability upper bound, not a universal VLM guarantee.
- Actors receive exact public context, one current public image per decision and compressed public action history. Earlier images are not reattached. Earlier edit bodies become hashes; current saved source and any different active source remain complete. Public constant contract/source prefixes may be cached by the provider; payload content and cache usage are recorded. All profiles use the same action interface and choose their own experiments/tests. Native and hosted inference compute are not equalized. Hosted outputs are capped at 4,096 tokens, low reasoning; Codex uses low effort and its native output limit.
- Primary outcome: submitted full protected pass. Secondary: fraction of protected checks passed, actual task reward, executed actions, schema/runtime failures, post-final-activation chosen tests, mismatches encountered and submissions without new tests. Wrong code does not alone identify a seeing failure.
- Statistical unit: distinct world, not grading case or action. Report per-family and balanced aggregate rates, 95% intervals, exact paired McNemar comparisons against Sol and Holm correction for four planned comparisons. Pairwise analyses use completed matching worlds and disclose coverage plus worst/best bounds for unobserved worlds. Stratified bootstrap over worlds gives paired effect intervals. No small-sample global model ranking.
- Prospective power: exact McNemar mixture with 40% discordant pairs gives 93.4% power at n=120 to detect a 20-point difference at alpha=.05, or 82.6% at conservative alpha=.0125. This depends on the stated alternative and full paired coverage. n=30 family results have substantially weaker power; smaller differences and hosted censored subsets can remain inconclusive.
- Hosted allowance: one shared hard $6 ledger including preflights, conservative reservations and unresolved charges. Qwen has a $2 sublimit to preserve Sonnet allowance. There is no promise that this cap buys 120 Sonnet episodes. Stop before an unreserved call; report budget coverage explicitly. GPT sign-in usage is separate.
- Recovery: persist decisions and idempotent action requests before execution. Retain all failed invocations, prefixes and immutable source/bank seals. Only infrastructure-interrupted decisions may be retried; valid model decisions are never regenerated. Model malformed output consumes an invalid action rather than being excluded as infrastructure.

Finite tests can establish concrete agreements and reject tested exploits. They cannot prove the absence of all grader bugs or reward hacks. Benchmarking frozen weights cannot establish a training benefit. That third claim requires the later GPU experiment with frozen checkpoints, matched inference settings and an untouched evaluation set.
